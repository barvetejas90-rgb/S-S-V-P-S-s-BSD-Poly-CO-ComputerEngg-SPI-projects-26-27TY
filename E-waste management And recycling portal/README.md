
E-Waste Management and Recycling Portal

Overview

This repository contains educational and research materials related to e-waste management and recycling, including a presentation, research papers, and a detailed report.

Contents

• Presentation: Information about e-waste management, environmental impacts, recycling methods, and sustainable solutions.

• Research Papers: Studies on electronic waste disposal, recycling technologies, environmental concerns, and waste management practices.

• Report: Detailed information about e-waste management, key challenges, research findings, and recommendations.

Objectives

• Understand the importance of e-waste management.
• Raise awareness about the environmental impact of electronic waste.
• Explore proper disposal, reuse, and recycling methods.
• Study sustainable waste management practices.
• Promote responsible electronic waste disposal.

About E-Waste

Electronic waste (e-waste) refers to discarded electrical and electronic equipment, such as computers, mobile phones, televisions, printers, and other electronic devices.

Improper disposal of e-waste can harm the environment and human health. Proper collection, reuse, and recycling help reduce pollution and recover valuable materials.
